[14:00] Aryan: Emergency meeting called. We have a critical bug

letters. The frontend is sending the email exactly as the user

[14:04] Rahul: I checked the database. The email column is using
a case-sensitive collation. So user@Gmail.com and user@gmail.com
are being treated as different entries. This is the root cause.

[14:06] Priya: This is a schema oversight on my part. The email
field should have been defined with a case-insensitive collation
or we should have enforced lowercase normalization at the
application layer. I take responsibility for this.

[14:08] Aryan: We are not here to assign blame. We need a fix
in the next two hours. What are our options?

[14:10] Rahul: Option one is to alter the email column collation

downtime of about 10 minutes.

[14:12] Priya: Option two is to add a database trigger that
normalizes all incoming emails to lowercase before insert and
update. No downtime required but adds complexity.

[14:14] Sneha: Option three is to fix it at the frontend by
converting all email inputs to lowercase before sending to the API.
This is the fastest fix but only solves the symptom, not the root cause.
